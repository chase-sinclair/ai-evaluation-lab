costs — Amounts in USD. Billed is invoiced cash, effective recognizes amortized expense, contracted is eligible on-demand price times quantity, list is undiscounted. Usage comparisons use category usage. Effective includes all categories unless explicitly limited to usage.

variance — SKU changes use realized contracted usage dollars per metered unit. Bridge volume at base mix and price, then mix at base regional prices, then prices at current regional units. Credits' contribution to a billed change uses signed credit change divided by signed total change.

output — Completed transactions are project-day outputs. Aggregate numerator and denominator independently before dividing. Missing daily output is zero when assessing activity. Undefined division returns NULL. Quarter comparisons use ratios of sums.

allocation — Use usage day and half-open owner intervals. Split direct effective cost by team share then center, unmatched goes to Unassigned (-1). Shared rows are removed from direct cost and distributed by calendar usage month and pool. Preserve all dollars including resource-less direct expense.

commitments — Eligibility uses contract account, SKU and term. Applications are contract-charge allocations. Savings equals covered units at dated SKU-region contracted rate less applied amortization. Service expense is applied plus unused; purchase cash uses billed purchase rows.
