# Choosing a reliability safeguard for an enterprise answer workflow

Start with a real decision: what will someone do differently after reading this answer, and how reversible is that action? A false filing deadline can cost more than a referral. A needless referral on a common status question can erase the value of self-service. Measure each against a grounded baseline allowed to clarify or decline.

## What the Lab has observed

On Alder Desk, pre-checking 24 packets yielded 24 correct packet classifications, higher estimated operating cost and longer modeled latency. It passed the shared P4 attribution error. Owner-reviewed AI-assisted grades found zero prevented unsupported answers and six redundant blocks. The owner retained the conservative P4 grade; there was no independent adjudication. Experiment 01 observed one repair in four live entity-resolution requests; routing put that case last in the pilot. Experiment 02 observed unchanged shared-task accuracy with larger catalogs and one wrong-direction payout proposal when release was missing. These findings inform which failures to investigate. None measures production ROI. [Phase A](experiment03_phase_a_review.md) · [Entity matching](portfolio/entity-matching.md) · [Tool access](portfolio/tool-catalog.md)

## Candidate deployment patterns

These are engineering hypotheses. For each client, sample representative requests, label real failure causes, estimate traffic and harm by workflow, then shadow a candidate against the current system before changing what customers see.

| Architecture | When it earns consideration | Prerequisites, trade-offs and production signals | Adoption or rejection evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard RAG** retrieves evidence and generates a cited answer, clarification or abstention. | Internal IT lookups, product support and reversible analyst queries with maintained sources. Weak fit for binding commitments without oversight. | Establish source owners, entitlements, versioning, retrieval monitoring and a clear fallback. One generation call. Audit unsupported claims and missing qualifiers, escalation rate, retrieval misses, p95 latency and cost per useful answer. | Keep it if a representative audit finds tolerable harm and checks mainly duplicate good abstentions. Reconsider if recurrent material errors remain despite adequate retrieval. |
| **Pre-generation gate** checks packet sufficiency and suppresses generation on BLOCK. | Support desks with frequent missing contract schedules or incomplete diligence files, provided a referral has a responsible recipient. Poor fit for predominantly supported lookups or errors introduced while writing. | Define packet-relative sufficiency, support for partial answers and clarification, immutable PASS forwarding and failure handling. Check cost on every query; generator spend saved only on blocks. Monitor useful answers lost, prevented errors, redundant blocks, referral backlog and serial latency. | Adopt if blocked cases contain costly errors the baseline really makes and prevented harm exceeds false-block cost. Reject if the baseline already requests missing documents or the gate mistakes ambiguity for insufficiency. |
| **Post-generation verifier** compares the actual candidate claims and citations to the packet before release. | Analysts quoting financial terms, diligence summaries and compliance guidance where a wrong number, actor or exception matters even with adequate evidence. Excessive for trivial reversible lookups if checks rarely change outcomes. | Need stable passage IDs, scoped authority rules, retention controls for the draft, a block fallback and independent audits of verifier misses. Always pay generator plus verifier. Monitor material errors caught, shared errors accepted, citation entailment, needless suppressions and tail latency. | Adopt when it catches consequential synthesis errors on adequate packets and the user can act on the referral. Reject when it repeats the generator's source mistake or discards many correct conditional answers. |
| **Selective verification** sends predefined high-risk requests to verification and serves the rest through standard RAG. Future option outside Phase B. | Mixed workloads, e.g. routine policy navigation alongside binding settlement terms. Insufficient where severity cannot be identified before serving the answer. | Predefine routing signals such as workflow, transaction type and consequence, then audit a random sample of bypassed traffic. Include routing cost, risk drift, missed high-severity cases and rollback. | Adopt only if routing captures enough costly errors out of sample while meeting latency targets. Reject if low coverage of risky cases or monitoring cost consumes the savings. |

**Fix the failure at its source.** When a signed amendment and a draft conflict, attach scope, status and effective dates during ingestion and enforce precedence. If signed peers conflict without resolution, refer to the document owner. If the governing document never reaches the packet, measure retrieval recall and repair indexing, access filters or ranking. A checker cannot recover an absent fact. For consequential writes, validate identity, permission, target and action direction in an execution control. Escalate unresolved ambiguity, exceptions requiring judgment and high-impact commitments to a named human with authority to decide. Log the evidence and final disposition.

## Diagnose the mechanism before selecting the intervention

Use a sample of real questions with the retrieved packet, generated reply and eventual disposition. Ask the operator to point to the first step where a correct decision became impossible. The following choices are hypotheses for a client pilot.

| Observed mechanism and example | First intervention and boundary | Prerequisite and operating trade-off | Evidence that reverses the choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Service-desk tickets get correct cited answers; existing abstentions already reach the right queue. | Keep standard RAG. Grounding supports routine advice; generation can still misstate an actor or deadline. | Maintain current documents, baseline audits and a staffed fallback. Lowest inference overhead; measure repeat contacts after refusals. | Recurring consequential errors with a common, preventable cause. |
| Missing diligence consents lead the baseline to assert approval. | Pre-gate packets for documentary sufficiency. Can suppress a guess; cannot determine whether consent exists outside the packet. | Define acceptable proof and own the referral queue. Pay a check on every question, save generation only on blocks; inspect useful partial answers lost. | Most blocks repeat good baseline abstentions or overload counsel with unnecessary reviews. |
| Financial summaries cite the right packet but substitute gross leverage for covenant leverage. | Post-check the generated claim against its definition and operands. Can withhold a wrong ratio; cannot recover missing cash data or supply a corrected memo. | Versioned evidence and accountable release path. Adds a serial call even on correct drafts; false blocks require analyst time. | Material substitutions pass both stages, or correct qualifications are routinely withheld. |
| Errors concentrate in binding credit terms while routine lookups dominate volume. | Pilot selective verification by predefined workflow or consequence. Protects covered traffic; misrouted cases bypass the verifier. | Reliable routing labels, bypass sampling and rollback. Pay routing overhead and verification on selected requests; track delays and escalations in both paths. | Audits find material errors dispersed across bypass traffic or routing costs absorb the savings. |
| The current rider exists in the repository but never reaches the claims packet. | Repair indexing, access-aware retrieval or ranking; measure governing-source recall. Better retrieval cannot guarantee faithful synthesis. | Labeled source IDs and entitlement tests. Index work and retrieval latency may rise; fewer avoidable desk referrals can offset them. | Governing passages are already retrieved and failures persist in the generated text. |
| Signed schedules conflict on scope or effective date in compliance answers. | Encode document authority and dates; route unresolved peers to a named owner. Rules can resolve precedence only where the business has established it. | Maintained metadata, version history, human queue SLA. Filtering can hide useful evidence; unresolved cases incur delay and labor. | Audit shows stale metadata or unnecessary escalations despite a uniquely governing source. |
| A support agent proposes the opposite payout action with valid fields. | Use deterministic identity, permission and action-direction checks before execution. These can veto a write; they cannot grade the accompanying explanation. | Trusted identity, structured authorized intent and deny path. Adds policy maintenance and denied-action handling; measure false denials and unsafe proposals intercepted. | If intent cannot be bound to a trusted instruction, require human authorization for that write. A mandatory authorization control cannot be waived on model accuracy alone. |
| A due-diligence exception requires risk acceptance even after the facts are established. | Send the evidence and open decision to an authorized human. Review can resolve a discretionary decision and assign accountability; reviewers can still err. | Named owner, evidence trail, response deadline and appeal path. Measure queue age, labor per case, reversals and unnecessary escalations. | If decisions consistently follow a codifiable policy, automate the bounded checks and retain human review for exceptions. |

### Three illustrative customer advisories

- **Treasury reporting.** Trace each incorrect memo figure to its packet. If the cash split was available but the draft repeatedly used gross debt, shadow a verifier on the same drafts. Have analysts count material errors caught, correct memos withheld and added p95 time before release. If the cash split was absent, pilot retrieval of the reconciliation first and measure its coverage.
- **Acquisition diligence.** Review a sample of closed checklist items with counsel. If outgoing requests were treated as executed consents, shadow a sufficiency gate that requires evidence of the lender’s disposition while allowing a useful status update. Compare false clearances prevented with baseline abstentions duplicated, supported status updates lost and counsel minutes per referral.
- **Internal incident response.** When two approved rosters disagree, test a roster-owner reconciliation and explicit conflict referral on past handovers. Measure confirmed primary assignments, time to reach the duty manager and unnecessary referrals on clean rosters. If discrepancies disappear but the assistant still quotes the secondary as primary, shift the pilot to answer verification.

## Illustrative economics for a client workshop

Use costs per 1,000 representative requests in the same period. Let **E** be material unsupported answers actually prevented relative to standard, **L** useful answers newly suppressed, and **H** additional human reviews. Expected net benefit is **E × expected loss per error − L × user cost per needless refusal − H × handling cost − incremental inference − latency cost − allocated operations cost**. Use severity-weighted loss including the probability that someone acts on the answer. Measure handling labor separately from user delay to avoid double counting. If the safeguard introduces new errors, deduct their expected losses.

For illustration only, suppose a prevented error avoids $40 in expected loss, eight needless refusals cost users $2 each, ten additional reviews cost $4 each, inference adds $1, latency adds $3 and monitoring adds $20 per 1,000. Added cost is $80. Two prevented errors break even; three yield $40 net under those assumptions. If expected loss is $10, eight preventions are needed to break even. A referral following an already correct abstention earns zero avoided loss. For a pre-gate subtract generator cost avoided on actual blocks; for a post-check charge verification on every candidate. Replace every assumption with client data and consider tail harm and mandatory controls independently of average dollars.
