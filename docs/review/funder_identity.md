# Funder identity worksheet · ROR v2.6

Give the reviewer this worksheet by itself. Work in a copy. The target is an active organization in the April 14, 2026 registry. Record enough evidence for another reviewer to repeat your search. A single identified human reviewer is acceptable for this development pilot.

First, search the full pinned registry using names, translations, acronyms and geographic clues. Check parent, successor and inactive records. The local command below searches the names, aliases and identifiers of every record and verifies the registry checksum. Try several searches. An empty search or an absent offered candidate alone cannot establish a no-match. The searchable CSV is available in the [pinned v2.6 archive](https://github.com/ror-community/ror-data/blob/26cf3dab9a740396bb5420b1f8f30d2e97c0149c/v2.6-2026-04-14-ror-data.zip).

For each decision enter MATCH with a full ROR URL, NO_SUPPORTED_MATCH when a documented registry search supports that conclusion, or INSUFFICIENT when the evidence leaves the identity unresolved. Leave the ID blank for the last two decisions. Include records checked, competing identities or search outcomes, your name and ISO date.

After recording all eight full-registry decisions, open the [offered candidate lists](funder_offers.md). For each offered-set result enter the matching offered URL, NONE if no offered ID matches, or UNCERTAIN. An identifiable full-registry organization can be absent from its candidate list. Keep your initial identity decision intact.
