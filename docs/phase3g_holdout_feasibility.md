# Independent Crossref holdout feasibility

**Retain 200 families as a conditional target. Proceed only to a 40-family blinded feasibility review if the owner approves the process.** The automated frame contains 416 positive-label families and 335 empty-annotation families. There is enough numerical headroom for 150 positives and 50 no-matches. The binding uncertainty is whether two reviewers can establish a single identity or a supported registry absence and clear family overlap. This audit certified **zero holdout labels**, selected zero families and made zero model calls.

The first screen excludes 146 rows for multiple, alternate or inconsistent normalized annotations, 851 for identifier/name-length/token/program rules, 27 for a missing or inactive target, and 526 empty rows without a qualifying organization term. Counts are first-exit, not overlapping flags. Active-singleton and organization-term filters carry over from development. They favor recognizable organizational wording, particularly English terms in the source-empty stratum. All numbers refer to this restricted frame.

**Family checks.** The script connects shared ROR IDs, normalized names, sorted name-family keys, parenthesized acronyms and accent-folded spelling. It checks geographic prefixes and suffixes against country, subdivision and locality names in pinned ROR. It follows parent, child, predecessor and successor chains in either direction, including through inactive records. Shared umbrella organizations can remove otherwise distinguishable branches. The second pass adds canonical, alternate, acronym and multilingual label forms for exposed and source-linked IDs, and checks source spelling variants against forms across the registry. A collision connects components for exclusion; it does not establish identity. Comma-delimited names, short acronyms and geographic stripping can overmerge. Unlisted translations, transliterations, historical relationships and internal geographic qualifiers can still split a real family. Eleven otherwise clear empty rows had possible active-name hits and were withheld. Zero mixed components remained at the final counting step.

**What the count supports.** At full-frame scale, at least 36.1% of positive candidates and 14.9% of source-empty candidates would have to survive review to reach 150/50. Within the proposed review ceilings of 225 and 100 families, the corresponding yields are **66.7%** and **50%**. At 75% and 60% acceptance, review would require roughly 200 and 84 nominations. At 60% and 40%, it would require roughly 250 and 125, beyond both ceilings. These are break-even scenarios, not observed acceptance rates. No defensible nonzero lower bound on the number of truly independent, verified families follows from string and graph checks alone. The eight previously reviewed development no-matches do not estimate holdout yield.

## Decision gates and deliverables

The decision applies only to this sampling design. Withholding source-empty records that resemble active names can remove genuinely difficult no-matches as well as questionable annotations. The relationship graph can likewise favor stand-alone organizations over large affiliated groups. Keep these exclusions visible in recruitment reporting. After freeze, count any recurring distractor IDs in offered lists separately from target-family overlap. Repeated distractors do not justify dropping low-scoring families or retrieval misses.

1. Review the [proposed sampling manifest](phase3g_sampling_manifest.json) and [blinded workflow](phase3g_reviewer_workflow.md). If approved, a custodian reconstructs the committed frame, draws 20 nominations per source stratum, and hides source stratum during review. This is a review stage with no model requests.
2. Report eligible yield and uncertainty per stratum, label and overlap exclusions, reviewer disagreements, minutes per record and remaining capacity. Continue only after a separate decision on those counts and reviewer capacity. If the 150/50 target cannot be met at the agreed standard, stop or prospectively revise sample size and precision goals. Preserve the same exclusions.
3. Freeze an adjudicated cohort and two policy rankings only after clearance. Even 200 families may resolve only fairly large effects. Fifty queries per arm need at most 100 distinct responses for both policies' absolute outcomes and potentially fewer for the paired difference alone. Exact overlap is unknown until the cohort is frozen. [Preregistered comparison, precision and acquisition plan](phase3g_protocol.md)
