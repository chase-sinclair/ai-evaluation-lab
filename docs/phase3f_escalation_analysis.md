# When should a funder name go to the LLM?

**Keep the frozen escalation ranking as the reference. Compare it with the top-two score margin on an untouched holdout before changing it.** Among 38 development queries, the frozen ranking sent four correctable baseline errors into the first nine slots. Margin alone captured three there, but reached all five by slot 19. The four-query pilot exposed a weakness of the reference rule. It also understated how well the rule found errors early elsewhere in development.

## Why CNPq ranked last in the pilot

The retriever scores each active ROR organization by its best matching name, alias, label or acronym using normalized character-trigram Dice similarity. It sorts distinct IDs by descending score with ROR ID breaking ties. The baseline can assert only the first ID. It links when both the leading score (t) reaches 0.75 and its lead over second place (g) reaches 0.03. Otherwise it abstains. Calibration maximized correct decisions on these same 38 queries over 15 threshold combinations. Ties favored fewer wrong links, then stricter thresholds. [Retrieval and calibration](phase3d_crossref_resolution.md)

The acquisition ranking uses `abs(round(min(t − 0.75, g − 0.03), 8))`, ascending, with lexical query ID breaking ties. On the link side, a small value means one requirement barely passed. On the abstention side, the **largest failed requirement** sets the distance. A weak leading score can therefore push a close contest late. Distance measures the decision rule's slack. It has no fitted relationship to the probability of an error or to the benefit of sending the query to the LLM. [Frozen decision code](../experiments/entity_matching/ror/core.py)

For **“CNPq process”**, the leading organization was Center for Process Studies (US). Its alias `ctr4process` scored **0.52173913**. The Brazilian agency Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico came second: its acronym `CNPq` scored **0.50000000** against the entire deposited string. The score shortfall was **0.22826087**, versus only **0.00826087** for the margin. The baseline therefore abstained with distance **0.22826087**. The four pilot distances in increasing order were Pakistan **0.0306**, Hong Kong **0.1500**, La Laguna **0.1700** and CNPq **0.2283**. Their full development ranks were **6, 23, 26 and 30**. CNPq had the weakest leader in all 38 and the seventh-smallest margin. The frozen candidate list already contained its annotated ID, `03swz6y49`, and the live response selected it. [Frozen candidate evidence](../experiments/entity_matching/ror/dataset.json) · [Four observed decisions](phase3e_live_findings.md)

The frozen pilot had **3/4 correct** at 0%, 25% and 50% escalation, and **4/4** at 100%. The first two escalations repeated correct baseline decisions. Replaying those same four observed responses retrospectively, margin-first and abstentions-first would each have reached CNPq at slot two, and weakest-leader-first at slot one. These three alternatives gain credit for the same single repair. The other 34 queries have no live outcome.

## Where the six baseline errors came from

Baseline accuracy was **32/38**: 18/18 registry-form names, 8/12 alternative wordings and 6/8 reviewed no-matches. Recall@5 was **29/30** for names with a target. The baseline selected the correct ID for **26/29** retrieved positives. Of nine abstentions, six were correct. Three wrong assertions included one wrong ID on a positive, so four true links were missed overall.

| Escalation rank | Query | Baseline error and what escalation could change |
|---:|---|---|
| 2 | DDL Diagnostics | Asserted CS Diagnostics. The shared word favored the wrong leader. DDL Diagnostic Laboratory was offered fourth. |
| 3 | Natural Science Foundation of Shaanxi Province, China | Asserted the national foundation. Shared wording obscured the provincial qualifier. Correct action under the reviewed label was abstention. |
| 4 | Natural Science Foundation of Jiangxi Province of China | Same wrong national organization, different provincial qualifier. These two related errors give this failure type extra weight. Correct action was abstention. |
| 5 | German Ministry for Education and Research | Abstained. The Swedish, Norwegian and Estonian organizations share the leading ministry name. The German organization's English alias and country support the fifth candidate. |
| 25 | Hoffmann - LaRoche Pharmaceuticals | Abstained. Pharmaceutical aliases dominated the offered list. The Swiss target ranked **eighth**. A valid response could avoid a false link but could never resolve this query correctly. |
| 30 | CNPq process | Abstained. A generic word competed with a distinctive acronym. The target was offered second. |

All six errors had a margin below 0.10, **as did ten correct decisions**. All 22 other decisions were correct. The eight no-match labels had one human registry review with AI-assisted research. That review distinguished absence of support in pinned ROR from absence among the five candidates. Small counts, related names, constructed class proportions and calibration on this cohort limit generalization. ## What equal review budgets buy offline

The unit below is a **baseline error that can be corrected with the offered actions**. Count an error when its true ID is offered or its reviewed label calls for abstention. Use labels only to evaluate each finished ordering. A correct baseline decision sent for escalation can regress. An unoffered target can never count as a repair.

| Ordering, ties by query ID | Correctable errors reached in 9 slots (25%) | In 19 slots (50%) | Correct baseline decisions exposed, 9 / 19 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Frozen distance, smallest first | 4 | 4 | 5 / 15 |
| Top-two margin, smallest first | 3 | 5 | 5 / 13 |
| Leading score, smallest first | 1 | 5 | 7 / 13 |
| Baseline abstentions first, then frozen distance within each group | 2 | 5 | 6 / 13 |
| Uniform random selection, exact expectation | 1.18 | 2.50 | 7.58 / 16.00 |

At 0 slots all orderings yield the measured baseline of 32/38. At 38 slots they expose all five repair opportunities, 32 correct decisions and one retrieval miss. Even a perfect offered-set selector tops out at **37/38**. At both intermediate budgets the frozen ordering tops out at **36/38**. Those ceilings assume every selected correctable error is repaired and every correct decision is preserved.

Random reference: 20,000 uniform permutations without replacement, seed 31006, nested prefixes of the same draw, with budgets rounded down. For a population of size (N) with (M) eligible errors the expected count at budget (k) is `k × M / N`. Simulated means for correctable errors were **1.196** and **2.501**. Central 95% ranges over allocations were **0–3** and **1–4**. These ranges describe random selection on these fixed names. They do not quantify generalization. With all four pilot queries removed, the frozen rule found **4/4** remaining correctable errors at both 8 and 17 slots. Narrowest margin found **3/4** and **4/4**. Thus the challenger’s extra repair opportunity at 50% on the full set comes entirely from CNPq. None of the comparisons warrants a production rule change. ## Proposed holdout and decision rule

1. **Define a new cohort before scoring it.** Target 200 distinct organization/name families from the pinned release, provisionally 150 positive and 50 reviewed no-match, at most one query per family. Use a preregistered seeded draw within label strata. Exclude all 579 quarantined source rows and audit cross-split overlap in IDs, offered organizations, aliases, translations, acronyms, parent/successor relationships and NIL name families. Keep all 38 analyzed queries in development. Have two reviewers adjudicate against the full pinned registry, blinded to retrieval, model outputs and each other. Record source labels, disagreements, exclusions and achieved counts. Keep retrieval misses. If the independent pool is too small, report the shortfall before freezing or calling. There is no certified holdout yet.
2. **Lock the paired comparison.** Freeze the existing retriever, five displayed candidates and their order, baseline thresholds, lexical-ID tie break, prompt, model/settings, family partition, ranking code and complete request hashes. Compare frozen distance with smallest margin as the sole challenger. Margin directly tests the close-contest explanation and requires no fitted parameters. Keep random selection as a reference. Prespecify one primary contrast: difference in end-to-end correct decisions at the same 25% call budget (50 of 200). Report 0%, 50% and 100% secondarily. Absolute budgets are based on the final frozen cohort. No threshold tuning or strategy selection on holdout results.
3. **Measure repairs and regressions on the same queries.** Reuse one response per query for both policies and for random replay. Report net correct decisions, discordant counts and a paired family bootstrap interval that recomputes each ranking's fixed-budget prefix per resample. Also report retrieval Recall@5, selection conditional on retrieval, false links on no-match, incorrect abstentions, asserted-link precision/recall/F1 and breakdowns by source stratum. Retain invalid responses and failures with the frozen baseline fallback. Separate token usage, latency, reserve, cost estimated from usage, and any billed cost. Report net additional correct resolutions per additional dollar versus baseline and across budgets when the denominator is positive. A target of 200 limits spend; precision depends on the number of discordant families. Report an inconclusive result if the interval spans meaningful benefit and harm. Estimates apply to the frozen class mix.
4. **Stage any later execution.** Acquire at most 200 distinct responses in total, sequentially, with zero retries. The first 20 would be an integrity checkpoint within the 200. Inspect only prespecified operational checks there, without examining comparative accuracy or revising policies. An 8,000-byte maximum canonical body plus 256 framing tokens and a 1,024-output-token ceiling would reserve **$0.002576 per call** at Phase 3E's historical conservative rates ($0.25/M input, $0.50/M output). That is **$0.05152** for the first 20 or **$0.51520** for 200; proposed inclusive caps **$0.055** and **$0.55**. These figures are planning bounds. Recheck prices, model availability and exact body-level reserves before any authorization. Retain the pinned 30-minute cache policy, standard service, immutable receipts and one-shot guard. Abort on integrity, coverage, model/tier, cache/usage, output, provider, or budget failures; disclose any truncated acquisition and leave unobserved policy outcomes unknown. The study needs owner review of cohort independence, adjudications, metric and budget before dispatch. [Prior pricing assumptions and controls](phase3e_readiness.md)
