# Blinded recruitment and adjudication

**Approval boundary.** No holdout worksheet has been populated. This procedure needs an owner-approved custodian, two independent reviewers and an adjudicator for disagreements. The first proposed release is 40 nominations. No model access is needed.

1. **Custodian prepares the queue.** Verify source and registry digests, the historical quarantine, this audit revision and the two frame membership hashes. Reproduce components, use the seeded family ordering and representative rule in the [manifest](phase3g_sampling_manifest.json), and keep the annotated queue inaccessible to the analysts and reviewers. Combine both strata and shuffle presentation by SHA-256 of UTF-8 `crossref-3g-blind-v1/<seq_no>`. Assign opaque IDs. Present only the deposited name and pinned registry search access. Hide Crossref ID and label, stratum, candidate list and scores, baseline, policy ranks, prior reviews and model output. Split reviewers may consult the exposure ledger solely for independence checks after identity worksheets are sealed.
2. **Two independent identity investigations.** Each reviewer records opaque ID, date, reviewer, registry version, exact search terms and variants, consulted ROR IDs, active/historical status, country, relevant relationship paths, supporting evidence and decision. Search display names, aliases, acronym expansions, language variants, place names, agency/subunit and predecessor/successor names across the full pinned registry. Use archived institutional evidence with a dated citation when needed to distinguish identities. Current web pages alone cannot establish registry membership at the pin. Reviewers work separately and sign their worksheets before comparison. Record research assistance if used; never count one person's assisted review as two.
3. **Use three decisions.** `LINK(active ROR ID)` requires one identifiable referent and evidence connecting the deposited wording to that record. `NO_SUPPORTED_ACTIVE_MATCH` requires an identifiable funder, documented full-registry searches and reasons plausible hits fail on identity, remit, geography or status. `INSUFFICIENT` covers unexplained acronyms, vague attributions, mixed sponsors, missing evidence and unresolved historical mapping. A search returning zero hits or a target outside five candidates alone establishes neither identity nor no-match.
4. **Resolve and verify independence.** A third reviewer adjudicates disagreements blind to the source label and model information, with both sealed evidence trails. Retain originals, final rationale and disagreement type. Exclude unresolved cases. The custodian then reconciles against the unaltered Crossref annotation; exclude source-discordant cases from this preregistered cohort and log them for separate study. Before inclusion independently compare against all development exposures and other nominated families using registry identifiers, geographic variants, acronyms, translations, alternate names and relationship chains. Exclude development collisions. Merge related within-holdout families using the manifest's deterministic precedence; exclude unresolved or conflicting components. Recheck collisions globally before freeze. Never select, remove or replace a name because of ranking, retrieval failure, predicted difficulty or model behavior.
5. **Log recruitment and stop.** Retain each nomination and first exclusion reason. Review one representative per family. For each stratum record attempted and accepted families, initial agreement, adjudicated and unresolved counts, exclusions and evidence time. Report Wilson 95% intervals for accepted fractions as approximate yield diagnostics. If even the upper yield bound projects a shortfall within the remaining review ceiling, stop for redesign. Otherwise submit the 40-case results for a continuation decision; broad intervals warrant a bounded second batch, not automatic authorization. No substitutions across strata. Unreviewed records stay sealed. Freeze the final crosswalk, exclusion and overlap ledger, reviewer receipts and blinded query hash before handing queries without labels to retrieval.

**Staffing estimate.** Allow 8–12 minutes per reviewer per positive nomination and 15–25 per source-empty nomination, plus 15–30% for custody, translations, overlap audits and dispute resolution. The first 20+20 need about **18–32 person-hours** including overhead. At the 225+100 ceilings, budget **127–225 person-hours** in total, including the first wave. These are planning assumptions. Re-estimate from logged first-wave times before recruiting further.
